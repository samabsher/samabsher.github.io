About

I study what policies actually do to the people they touch — border enforcement, regime change, revolutions, school quality — using the tools of causal inference: synthetic control, regression discontinuity, and difference-in-differences.

I am a Senior Consultant at Research & Planning Consultants, LP. Before that I spent six years as an economist at RAND, where I also taught microeconomics and cost-benefit analysis at the RAND School of Public Policy. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Oklahoma.

Selected research

Fences and Fortunes: The Effect of Enhanced Border Enforcement on El Paso

with Robin Grier and Kevin Grier · under review

In 1993, Operation Hold the Line put a wall of Border Patrol agents between El Paso and Ciudad Juárez. Crossings collapsed — and so did incomes, house values, and business growth on the U.S. side. The costs to El Paso swamped the benefits.
